Meet Candy

A little mascot that lives at the top of your screen and watches your memecoins, so you don't have to stare at charts all day.

Free and open source · Windows, macOS and Linux

Open this page on a computer to try Candy live, or watch the tour.

Every coin you follow, at a glance

Add a token by name, address or DexScreener link and Candy follows it: price, market cap, liquidity, volume, buys and sells, with a tiny chart.

  • Solana
  • BSC
  • Ethereum
  • Base
  • Robinhood Chain

It tells you when it matters

Set alerts on price, market cap or sell pressure. When one hits, Candy jumps, sparkles or sulks right on your screen, with a notification you can act on.

Your wallet, read-only

Paste a wallet address and see what you hold, where you got in and your PnL, including today's profit. Solana and EVM wallets. Candy only reads: no keys, no connect button.

It keeps you honest

A daily loss limit, a trade cap, a cooldown after a loss and a nudge before you chase a pump. Your rules, kept by a friend who never gets tired.

Ask Candy

An optional AI chat on your own OpenAI key. Drop a project's site on it for a quick check of the domain, the hosting and any red flags. Or just talk to it: Ctrl+Alt+M, ⌘⌥M on a Mac.

Dress it up

37 hats, 36 faces, 24 colours and 15 costumes, Floki the viking shiba and Max Headroom included. Candy speaks 10 languages, too.

Read-only by design

No keys, ever

Candy never asks for a seed phrase or a private key, and it never trades.

No account, no tracking

Your settings stay on your computer. No ads, no analytics.

Built in the open

Every download is built by GitHub from code you can read, and the Windows app is certified by Microsoft.
